# Primary sources and provenance

Research date: **20 September 2026**. Specifications are public reference inputs, not measurements from a physical device. Availability and browser support can change.

## Hardware and system layout

**Apple — iPhone Duo Technical Specifications**

https://www.apple.com/iphone-duo/specs/

Used for the continuous 7.6-inch inner OLED, 1878 × 2670 pixel resolution, 5.4-inch outer OLED, 1398 × 2034 pixel resolution, enclosure dimensions and orientation ratios. The simulator uses its own logical design coordinates, not an asserted UIKit point size.

**Apple Newsroom — Apple unveils iPhone Duo, 9 September 2026**

https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/

Used for the announcement and availability dates, side-oriented controls and dock, vertically oriented Dynamic Island, Today View, expanded Home Screen, Split View and app-pair/continuity concepts. The announced availability is 23 October 2026, with preorders on 16 October. The native product was not physically tested for this project. The manually controlled Book and Tabletop layouts are this prototype's design exploration, not verification of a specific native posture API.

## Application selection

**Apple App Store — Get the US’s Top Apps of 2025**

https://apps.apple.com/us/story/id1847717004

Selection: ChatGPT, Threads, Google, TikTok, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, Google Maps, Gmail and Google Gemini, in that order. This is the published U.S. free-iPhone download chart for 2025. It is not a claim about September 2026 global monthly active users or download rank.

## Web platform

**MDN — WebGPU API**

https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGPU_API

Reference for compute/render capabilities, browser support constraints and secure-context requirements. The actual implementation reports success or its Canvas fallback in the interface, rather than assuming availability from the user agent.

## Original material

All photographs/art-like images are procedural compositions produced by `scripts/generate_assets.py`: dunes, alpine landscape, coast, arches, orbital study and golden landscape. They are fictional scenes rather than documentary photographs. All three bundled films and their synthesized score are generated by the same script. The source WAV is included for regeneration.

The fictional Porto Alba city, places, graph, route data, people, messages, posts, articles, captions, sample weather and numerical engagement counters are original demonstration content. The city has no actual geographic coordinates or navigation validity.

Interface icons and familiar app-symbol interpretations are hand-authored inline SVGs. These are not extracted native app assets. No Apple wallpaper, native application binary, downloaded stock photograph, commercial film, third-party music recording or font file is bundled. System fonts are requested through CSS; they are not redistributed.

## Independence

The project is a browser interaction study, unaffiliated with Apple, OpenAI, Google, Meta or ByteDance. App and product names identify the workflows being explored. No third-party account, API or branded service is accessed by the demo.
